# Kitchen0.6.1 appliance addendum

Owner-reported equipment: multicooker, air fryer and waffle iron. These are request choices and chat routing options, not automatically written to every member profile.

Multicooker currently supports non-pressure steam only where the device manual permits it; appropriate steam insert/form and reservoir water per manufacturer instructions are required. Pressure-cooker modes are not supported without the specific model. Air-fryer poultry, fish and vegetables have separate methods. Waffles use known egg/cheese ingredients without implicit flour/sugar/oil. All results remain calculated drafts, not kitchen-tested recipes.

Device model, capacity and exact cooking programs are unknown. No universal appliance temperature, water volume or guaranteed duration is assigned. Existing food-doneness constraints and explicit inventory/calculation checks remain. See `docs/evidence/KITCHEN_APPLIANCES_ADDENDUM.json`.
